Publications

The lab's first papers are still ahead of it.

The Eremina Laboratory opens in January 2027 and has not yet published under its own name. What follows is Olga Eremina's work to date, from her doctoral and postdoctoral research — the foundation the lab's programs build on.

All papers, newest first. Entries marked in review are under revision at the named journal. Daggers mark equal contribution; asterisks mark corresponding authorship. A complete and current record is maintained on ORCID and Web of Science (ResearcherID N-3406-2016).
